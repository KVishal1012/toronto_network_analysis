# Five-person usefulness study

Record the candidate build hash, analysis version, browser/device, session date and anonymous P01–P05 identifier. Obtain permission to retain anonymous written answers. Do not collect names, contact details or home addresses. Store actual answers outside public assets. Do not invent responses.

Neutral introduction: “Please use this tool to investigate a neighbourhood issue. Tell us what you think the information means. Uncertainty and disagreement are useful feedback.” Do not explain the expected answers before each task.

1. Choose Downtown or Midtown and identify an area you would investigate further. Explain why, or why you would not select any area.
2. Compare two areas. Find a proposal's publisher source and date. Explain what the reported unit count and stage establish.
3. Change the distance and service period. Explain whether your initial priority changes and what the distance/service measures tell you.
4. Open Downtown 3D, inspect a recorded building, add a hypothetical volume and change its height or footprint. Explain which geometry is recorded and which is your design, and whether the design establishes development permission.
5. Attach a 3D view, write your own investigation question and produce a brief. Save and restore the scenario. Explain whether it helps a real community decision and what you would do next.

For each task retain verbatim answer, elapsed time, success/partial/failure, any facilitator intervention and observed problems. Separately ask: “What would you use instead?”, “What was missing?” and “Would you use this for an actual issue? Which one?”

Acceptance: at least four of five finish the workflow unaided; all five correctly distinguish proposals from construction and proximity from walking/capacity, and source geometry from hypothetical design. Retain failures, correct the interface and repeat on the revised build. Never turn a positive opinion into evidence of real civic action. Report completed briefs and task performance separately from external outcomes.

Recruitment is currently unsecured. If no sessions occur, the submission must state “User impact not yet validated.” AI reviews cannot satisfy this test.
