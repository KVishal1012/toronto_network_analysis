# Toronto Open Data Awards — draft, not submitted

**Project:** ASTER — Housing & Transit Explorer
**Category:** Open Data Applications; grassroots/self-funded subject to owner reconfirmation.
**Civic question:** Which areas with recorded housing proposals deserve closer investigation of nearby scheduled transit service?

ASTER helps community groups compare proposal quantities and scheduled TTC proximity in Downtown and Midtown. Users choose a region, test distance and time assumptions, inspect exclusions and source evidence, and create a brief containing their own investigation question. The product uses explicit rules instead of an opaque neighbourhood score.

The contribution is a reproducible comparison of planning evidence with scheduled service, with missing data and uncertainty visible. It does not establish future population, construction, transit capacity, walking routes or a service deficit. Numerical findings are pinned to specific source snapshots and dates.

**Evidence of usefulness:** developer tests and an independent computational source reread are available. User impact is not yet validated. Do not claim resident adoption, institutional use, improved service or policy outcomes without actual evidence.

## 90-second demonstration

1. Open Downtown at 800 m in the morning. Show the honest empty shortlist and explain that it does not prove adequate transit.
2. Select North York and compare two candidate areas. Explain that quantities are reported proposed units, not future homes or new riders.
3. Compare 400/800/1600 m and morning/evening/Saturday results. Open a planning record and its scheduled-service evidence.
4. Show excluded/unlocated records and explain what the analysis cannot assess.
5. Enter a concrete question about walking connections or capacity. Export/print a source-linked community brief and reload its pinned link.
