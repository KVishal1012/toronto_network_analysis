Subject: Separate permissions questions: active development applications and address points

We are preparing a free planning explorer, currently tested locally. A public version would host source records and attributes, search indexes, transformed display geometry, downloadable evidence and immutable historical snapshots. Browsers would fetch and may cache these files automatically when searching or opening evidence. We would identify the source, retrieval date and limitations and would not imply City endorsement.

**1. Active development applications — permission and applicable terms**

For [planning item 574287950c2c415fbcd2cbb37d617ba1](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=574287950c2c415fbcd2cbb37d617ba1), the item metadata prohibits copying or modification without written consent and also links the City's Terms of Use.

Please identify the controlling terms for this exact resource and whether written permission is required. Please confirm whether we may host and redistribute original records and attributes, transform geometry for display and distance calculations, offer JSON/CSV evidence downloads, and retain dated historical snapshots. Please specify required attribution, excluded fields or records, withdrawal/update obligations, and the source's application scope and update frequency.

**2. Address points — downstream agreement and delivery**

Separately, [address item 8efbfcb82a2e4373bea6013105ed1c8c](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=8efbfcb82a2e4373bea6013105ed1c8c) links the [Terms of Use](https://www.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/961c790805c14d8da258ec91bf4117e3/data). These permit distribution but require downstream recipients to agree to and be bound by the terms.

For the browser delivery described above, is a conspicuous terms notice and link sufficient before any address records load, or must each visitor explicitly accept first? If explicit acceptance is necessary, please describe the expected mechanism and whether it must cover direct file access, browser caching, downloadable exports and subsequent redistribution. Please also confirm whether source address records may remain in dated historical snapshots if the live dataset or terms change.

We will keep these resources unavailable until their separate permission and delivery requirements are resolved. We do not treat planning approval as construction or address/source geometry as surveyed boundaries.

Thank you.
